Domain Challenge: Why Off-the-Shelf OCR Fails

Standard OCR libraries (Tesseract, EasyOCR, and native PaddleOCR) achieve less than 40% accuracy on digital utility meters due to four distinct physical and visual characteristics:

Faceplate Clutter and Text Contamination Printed serial numbers, barcodes, utility brand logos, and manufacture dates distract general-purpose text detectors away from the active consumption display.
Segment Disconnection in 7-Segment LCDs Models trained on continuous printed text fracture unlit 7-segment character gaps into disconnected strokes, misclassifying digits into phonetic letters (such as 0 into N, or 8 into B).
Unit Marker Interference Adjacent measurement unit symbols (kWh, kW, MD) merge into the active digits, causing catastrophic 10x to 100x billing multiplier errors.
Strict Leading Zero Requirements Utility registers require verbatim leading zero preservation (e.g., 007888.54). Standard numerical parsers routinely strip leading zeros, invalidating billing ledger reconciliation.

Pipeline Architecture

STAGE 1

YOLOv11n LCD Detector (10.6 MB)

An anchor-free Feature Pyramid Network (FPN) trained on wide ambient shifts and tilted handheld angles. Letterboxes full images to 640x640, isolates the digital register, and discards all surrounding faceplate clutter with 100% false-positive rejection on non-meter scenes.

STAGE 2

Dynamic Aspect-Preserving Normalization (96px Canonical Height)

Extracted bounding boxes are scaled to a fixed canonical height of 96 pixels with dynamic aspect ratio preservation. This standardized resolution was proven via ablation to preserve sub-pixel decimal points across wide multi-digit registers without geometric distortion.

STAGE 3

PP-OCRv6 SVTR-CTC Recognizer (31 MB FP16 / 62 MB FP32)

Combines a lightweight PPLCNet visual backbone with Single Visual Token Representation (SVTR) attention. The classification head is strictly pruned from 6,000+ characters down to 11 tokens (digits 0 to 9 and decimal point), mathematically preventing unit icons and alphanumeric text from being hallucinated.

Dataset Taxonomy & Stratification Protocol

To guarantee real-world generalization across diverse field hardware, the dataset is structured across a tri-domain taxonomy encompassing 4,094 curated images:

Domestic (Set A)
Single-phase residential meters with green/blue backlit LCDs
Commercial (Set B)
Mixed commercial installations with dirty glass and dimmed segments
Industrial (Set C)
High-voltage three-phase meters with wide 5:1 multi-line registers

Strict Physical Meter Grouping: Splits are partitioned strictly by unique physical meter identifier rather than random image sampling. Images of any individual physical meter reside exclusively within train, validation, or the 150-meter held-out test split, ensuring unbiased evaluation on genuinely unseen utility equipment.
